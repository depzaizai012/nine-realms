# W01 — Verdant corruption intensity / Enemy Art Bible v1.1
Status: owner-approved visual art direction, 2026-11-07. Derived from Enemy Art Bible v1.0 and World Art Direction Bible v1.0.
 
**A LIVING SACRED FOREST BEING POISONED FROM WITHIN — NOT A DEAD FOREST.**
Global 0–5 means visual corruption only, never a gameplay condition; age, Dark and poison are independent of corruption. Base palette living forest green (#234C3C), moss (#50795C), bark (#70563E), leaf jade (#79C89A), old gold (#C9B16D). Selective muted violet and black sap only where a specific enemy is actually corrupted.

| Enemy | Intensity | Art direction |
|---|---:|---|
