Ultimate timing is centralized: 300 ms entrance, 450 ms readable hold, 500 ms element power, 100 ms visual hit stop, 240 ms result impact, 350 ms feedback and 360 ms exit. A presentation with active results lasts about **2.3 seconds**. A separate 850 ms enemy death animation can follow a killing ultimate before the battle resumes.

Normal identities remain distinct: Aria has leaf orbits and a verdant burst; Fenrir has a fiery slash and ember trail; Rowan has a rock shard and dust; Sylva has a dark bolt, void trail and purple pulse; Pip has a gold-white star and sparkle trail. Ultimates amplify these identities through a verdant aura, fiery cleave, stone wall, void bloom and celestial pulse respectively. Ally heals show mint/gold plus particles, shields show gold diamond particles, and all results come from the unchanged battle engine.

Rendering layers separate board effects, projectiles, raised hero cards, ultimate art, floating numbers and the top HUD. The tutorial toast is dismissed when an action starts so it cannot cover the attacking avatar. Cinematic layout and raised-card bounds are checked at **390×844, 360×800 and 412×915**; screenshots are stored in `tests/artifacts/polish-*.png`.

Browser evidence includes an actual FIRE gesture, measured rise/return timing, selected-enemy damage, multiple sequential hero actions, correct line destruction sets and stagger order, unchanged non-matched cells, all five ultimate artwork choices, strong VFX, gold readiness, consumed energy, no overlap with the board, locked gestures during actions and AUTO remaining off throughout.
