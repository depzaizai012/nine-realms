All 12 engine tests and 6 browser tests pass. Additional engine coverage verifies invalid swap rollback, match-4 and match-5 creation, T-shaped bomb creation, special chain reactions, gravity/refill, ultimate energy gating, healing, revive and shield effects. A seeded fixture resolves **four cascades from one gesture** and records **one player turn and one enemy phase**. Chromium touch events verify a mobile swipe and pausing an in-flight resolution without additional HP changes.

Responsive screenshots are in `tests/artifacts/battle-{390,360,412}.png`. Browser assertions verify no page scrolling, five cards on one row, correct grid dimensions, visible production art, target selection, drag input, pause/resume and speed controls. VFX tests capture animation calls as well as inserted visual elements, avoiding missed assertions for effects that last only a few hundred milliseconds.

The automated 100-run smoke check won all 100 runs with random valid moves and immediate use of charged ultimates, averaging 33.7 turns. This checks that tutorial balance is forgiving under that policy. The target **90–95% new-player clear rate needs human playtesting** and is not claimed as measured.

Future boss encounters, advanced hazard gameplay and cinematic ultimate cut-ins are not part of this stage. The canonical boss records and Thornheart phase assets are registered; Thornheart does not appear in Stage 1-1.
